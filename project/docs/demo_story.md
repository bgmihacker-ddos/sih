# FORENSIGHT V2 Demo Scenario (SIH)

## 60-Second Pitch
"In a landscape where email phishing is the #1 vector for data breaches, analysts are overwhelmed by manual, fragmented analysis. FORENSIGHT converts raw email evidence into a structured, explainable threat-intelligence graph. We provide automated forensics, cross-case correlation, and deterministic risk scoring—empowering analysts to stop campaigns, not just individual emails, in seconds."

## 3-Minute Demo Sequence
1. **The Ingestion (0:00-0:45)**: Analyst logs in. Creates a new case for a suspicious threat. Uploads a raw `.eml` file.
2. **The Forensics (0:45-1:30)**: System automatically parses headers (detecting SPF failure), extracts IOCs (IPs/URLs), and runs the risk engine. Shows a risk score based *only* on evidence.
3. **The Intelligence (1:30-3:00)**: Analyst opens the "Infrastructure Graph". System pivots to a related case based on shared IP infrastructure, showing a possible ongoing campaign. Analyst generates a professional forensic report in a single click.
